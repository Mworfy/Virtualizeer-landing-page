01 · The theme

Isolated Together.

Rebuilding Connection in a Digital Age

Youth Speak Forum is an AIESEC initiative. When we analyzed the survey results, mental health emerged as the dominant concern at 78.3%, nearly double any other topic. Treating it alone risked feeling overdone. Looking closer, every other concern pointed to the same underlying crisis: disconnection.

We live surrounded by digital connections (notifications, social media, messages), yet feel lonelier, more insecure, and more divided than ever. We are “isolated together”: physically close to technology, but emotionally distant from one another.

This summit explores how to rebuild real bonds with ourselves, with each other, and with our communities in an age dominated by the digital.

05 · What we found

Every concern points back to disconnection.

Mental health leads at 78.3%, nearly double any other topic, and the rest of the survey maps the same crisis from different angles.

  1. 78.3%

    Mental health

    The central symptom of a society that is increasingly connected digitally, yet increasingly alone in reality.

  2. 60.4%

    Jobs & unemployment

    Economic uncertainty isolates people from social roles and from belonging to a productive community.

  3. 59.9%

    Fake news & misinformation

    Social media, designed to connect us, polarizes us into separate information bubbles.

  4. 43.2% / 42.4%

    Social divisions & inequality

    Technology often amplifies distance between groups, generations, and social classes.

  5. 20.1%

    Climate change

    Climate anxiety often stems from individual powerlessness and a lack of real collective action.

06 · FAQ

What is the Youth Speak Forum?

What is the Youth Speak Forum?

Youth Speak Forum is an official AIESEC youth summit that connects young people with partners, universities, NGOs, and decision-makers. Through surveys, forums, and projects, it turns youth perspectives into collective action. This is the official website and registration portal: ysf.aiesecus.org.

What is the Youth Speak Forum theme?

The current Youth Speak Forum theme is Isolated Together: Rebuilding Connection in a Digital Age. It explores how digital life can leave people connected online yet emotionally distant, and how to rebuild authentic bonds.

How do I register for Youth Speak Forum?

Register as a delegate on the official registration page at ysf.aiesecus.org/register.html. For partnership inquiries, open the partner form on this site. Questions: youthspeakforum@aiesecus.org.

Where is the official Youth Speak Forum website?

The official Youth Speak Forum website for this edition is https://ysf.aiesecus.org/. Use it for theme details, survey findings, and delegate registration.

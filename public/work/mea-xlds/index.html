About The Conference

Exchange and Leadership Development Seminar

MEA XLDS is the Middle East & Africa region's flagship Exchange and Leadership Development Seminar. This premier regional event brings together AIESECers from local committees across the MEA region for several days of transformative learning and collaboration.

As a standard AIESEC conference format, XLDS combines Exchange (bringing members from across countries and cultures together) with Leadership Development (focused personal and collective growth through sessions, workshops, and discussions) in a seminar-style learning environment.

Leadership Sessions & Strategic Workshops

Capacity building designed to challenge perspectives and build leadership capabilities

Regional Discussions & Strategy Reflection

Reflect on organizational cycles and contribute to regional priorities and impact

Exchange of Best Practices

Share knowledge and experiences across countries in the MEA region

Regional Unity & Networking

Build lifelong bonds and strengthen cross-cultural collaboration among future leaders
